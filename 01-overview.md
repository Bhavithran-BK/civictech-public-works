# 01 - Overview: DigOnce

**Track:** CivicTech - Improving Transparency and Coordination in Public Works Projects

**One-line pitch:** A live public map of every road, drain and pipeline project, plus a shared planner that stops departments from digging the same road twice, and a citizen verification loop that holds delays accountable.

## Problem we solve
- Residents can't see what is being built, by whom, or until when.
- Departments don't know each other's plans, so roads are dug up repeatedly.
- Stalled or poor work has no simple follow-up or accountability path.

## What makes it stand out

1. **Dig-Once conflict engine**
   When a department schedules work, the system checks whether another project is planned or was finished within X metres and Y months on the same road. It flags the clash and suggests a joint window.

2. **Citizen verification**
   Residents check in on a project with a geo-tagged photo ("work is happening" or "nobody is here"). Check-ins are verified by GPS distance and timestamps, and feed a **Project Health Score**.

3. **QR on site boards**
   Every project gets a QR code that opens its public page: purpose, authority, contractor, timeline, delay reason.

4. **Delay accountability**
   A delay can only be logged with a reason, and the new date shows on the public timeline. Stalled projects auto-escalate when there is no activity for N days.

## Success definition
A judge opens a public link, with no setup, and within 3 minutes sees: the map, a clash warning, a citizen report, a project flipping to "Stalled", and the authority dashboard.
